Gulab Jamun Recipe

Soft gulab jamun is all about the texture: smooth balls with no cracks, gentle frying and a light syrup that can travel into the centre. The dough should be soft enough to roll without pressure but never sticky.

Fry slowly rather than chasing a dark colour. Even golden browning gives the milk-based dough enough time to cook before it meets the syrup.

25 minPrep
25 minCook
16Pieces

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • Milk as needed
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 cardamom pods
  • Rose water
  • Oil or ghee for frying
  • Pistachios

How to Make Gulab Jamun

  1. Mix milk powder, flour, baking powder and ghee. Add milk a teaspoon at a time until a soft dough forms.
  2. Rest for 5 minutes, then roll small smooth balls. Fix any cracks with slightly damp fingers.
  3. Heat oil or ghee over medium-low heat. Fry slowly, turning gently, until evenly golden brown.
  4. For syrup, simmer sugar, water and cardamom for 5–7 minutes. Keep it light rather than very thick.
  5. Place warm jamuns into warm syrup and let them soak for at least 30 minutes.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Serve two jamuns warm with a small scoop of vanilla ice cream and crushed pistachio. The hot syrup, soft centre and cold creaminess make a simple dessert feel like a restaurant finish.

Serving

Serve warm or at room temperature. Gulab jamun is ideal for family dinners, celebrations and festive dessert tables.

Softness Tip

Do not knead the dough aggressively. Overworking it can make the cooked jamuns dense instead of tender.
