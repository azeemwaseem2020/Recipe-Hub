Gulab Jamun Recipe

Gulab jamun is a classic Pakistani and Indian dessert made from soft milk-based dough, fried gently and soaked in fragrant sugar syrup.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 3–4 tbsp milk
  • Oil for frying
  • 1.5 cups sugar
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 3 cardamom pods

Method

  1. Combine milk powder, flour and baking powder; rub in ghee.
  2. Add milk a little at a time until a soft dough forms. Rest 5 minutes.
  3. Roll into smooth small balls with no cracks.
  4. Simmer sugar, water and cardamom for 5–7 minutes.
  5. Fry the balls on low-medium heat until evenly golden.
  6. Transfer hot jamun to warm syrup and soak for at least 1 hour.

Tips

Do not make the dough stiff, keep the frying temperature moderate, and make smooth balls so they cook evenly.
