Kheer Recipe

Good kheer is creamy but not stodgy. The rice should soften into the milk gradually, releasing enough starch to make the pudding silky while the cardamom keeps the flavour fragrant.

Cook it in a heavy pot over gentle heat and stir regularly around the base. Remember that kheer thickens considerably as it cools, so stop cooking while it still looks a little looser than the final texture you want.

10 minPrep
60 minCook
6Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 1/3 cup basmati rice
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • Almonds & pistachios
  • Raisins
  • 1 tsp rose water
  • Saffron, optional

How to Make Kheer

  1. Rinse rice and soak for 10–15 minutes. Drain well.
  2. Bring milk to a gentle simmer in a heavy pot. Add rice and cook slowly, stirring often.
  3. When the rice is tender and the milk has reduced, add sugar and crushed cardamom.
  4. Continue cooking until creamy but still slightly loose. Stir more often near the end.
  5. Turn off the heat and add rose water and nuts. Serve warm or chill until cold.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Toast the pistachios and almonds for one minute in a dry pan before topping the kheer. The small amount of roasted aroma makes a simple dessert taste much more special.

Serve

Serve chilled for a firm pudding or slightly warm for a softer, flowing texture. It is a beautiful dessert for family dinners, Eid gatherings and weekend meals.

Texture Tip

Stop cooking before the kheer becomes very thick. It will continue thickening while it cools.
