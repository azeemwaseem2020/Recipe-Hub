Kheer Recipe

Kheer is a creamy South Asian rice pudding made with milk, rice, sugar and cardamom. It is popular in Pakistani and Indian homes and is especially loved at celebrations.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rice
  • 1 liter full-fat milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 2 tbsp chopped almonds or pistachios

Method

  1. Rinse and soak the rice for 15 minutes.
  2. Bring milk to a gentle simmer in a heavy-bottomed pot.
  3. Add rice and cook on low heat, stirring often.
  4. Once rice is soft and milk has thickened, add sugar and cardamom.
  5. Cook 5 more minutes, garnish with nuts and serve warm or chilled.

Tips for creamy kheer

Use full-fat milk, cook slowly, and stir the bottom regularly so the milk does not catch. Kheer thickens further as it cools.
