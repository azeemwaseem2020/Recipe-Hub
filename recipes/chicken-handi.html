Chicken Handi Recipe

Chicken handi is a rich, comforting curry where the tomato base, yogurt and cream come together around tender chicken. The name suggests a clay pot, but a heavy saucepan works perfectly at home.

The important step is bhunai: cook the tomato and spice mixture until concentrated before adding the creamy ingredients. This keeps the final curry flavourful instead of watery.

15 minPrep
35 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes or puree
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • Oil, chilies & coriander

How to Make Chicken Handi

  1. Heat oil in a heavy pan and brown the chicken lightly. Remove and keep aside.
  2. Cook onion until soft, then add ginger-garlic, tomatoes and spices. Bhuno until the mixture is thick and the oil starts to separate.
  3. Lower the heat. Add yogurt gradually while stirring so it blends smoothly into the masala.
  4. Return chicken and simmer until cooked through. Add a small splash of hot water if the sauce becomes too tight.
  5. Stir in cream, garam masala and green chili. Cook gently for 2–3 minutes and finish with coriander.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Warm the serving bowl before plating and spoon the curry into it immediately. The retained heat keeps the cream aroma and glossy surface alive for longer at the table.

Serve

Serve with butter naan, plain naan, roti or rice. A crisp salad and lemon provide a useful fresh contrast to the creamy curry.

Yogurt Tip

Add yogurt on low heat and stir continuously at first. This reduces the chance of splitting and gives a smoother gravy.
