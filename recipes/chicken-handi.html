Chicken Handi Recipe

Chicken handi is a creamy Pakistani chicken curry with tomatoes, yogurt and warming spices. It is ideal with naan, roti or rice.

Ingredients

  • 750 g chicken
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt and fresh coriander

Method

  1. Sauté onion in oil until lightly golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken; cook until the chicken changes color.
  3. Add tomatoes and spices and cook until soft and the oil begins to separate.
  4. Lower heat and stir in yogurt gradually.
  5. Cover and simmer until chicken is tender and the curry is creamy.
  6. Add garam masala and coriander before serving.

Serving tip

Serve hot with butter naan, roti or steamed rice. For a richer curry, use full-fat yogurt.
