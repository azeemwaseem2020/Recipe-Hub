Butter Chicken Recipe

Butter chicken is a creamy Indian curry made with tender chicken in a tomato-based sauce. This approachable version balances butter, cream and aromatic spices without making the method complicated.

Prep:15 min · Cook:30 min · Serves:4

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp garam masala and 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • Salt and fresh coriander

Method

  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, half the spices and salt for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Cook the chicken in a hot pan until browned and cooked through.
  3. Cook tomatoes with ginger-garlic paste until soft, then blend until smooth.
  4. Melt butter, add the sauce and remaining spices, and simmer for 8–10 minutes.
  5. Add chicken and cream. Simmer gently for 5 minutes and garnish with coriander.
Tip: Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer after adding cream to maintain a smooth texture.

Serve With

Serve with naan, roti or basmati rice. Try Butter Naan or return to all recipes.
