Butter Chicken Recipe

Butter chicken should feel silky, gently spiced and deeply savoury rather than simply heavy. In a home kitchen, the easiest way to build that flavour is to give the chicken some colour first, then make a smooth tomato base and finish it with butter, cream and a little dried fenugreek.

This recipe is designed for an ordinary evening when you want a special dinner without complicated restaurant equipment. Keep the final simmer gentle and the sauce will stay glossy and smooth.

20 minPrep
35 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chili powder or paprika
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander

How to Make Butter Chicken

  1. Mix chicken with yogurt, half the spices and salt. Rest for at least 20 minutes while you prepare the other ingredients.
  2. Heat a wide pan. Add a little oil and cook the chicken over medium-high heat until the outside gets browned and the chicken is cooked through. Remove it.
  3. In the same pan, cook tomatoes and ginger-garlic paste until soft and concentrated. Blend until smooth for a restaurant-style finish.
  4. Add butter and the blended sauce back to the pan. Stir in the remaining spices and simmer for 8–10 minutes until the sauce deepens in colour.
  5. Return the chicken, add cream and simmer gently for about 5 minutes. Taste for salt and finish with coriander.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: For a deeper flavour without extra cream, crush a small pinch of dried fenugreek leaves between your palms and stir them in during the final minute. The aroma becomes noticeable as soon as the hot sauce hits the table.

Serving & Storage

Serve hot with butter naan, plain naan or steamed basmati rice. Store leftovers chilled in a covered container and reheat slowly; a splash of water or milk can loosen the sauce without changing its character.

Kitchen Tip

Do not boil the curry hard after adding cream. A gentle simmer gives a smoother sauce and keeps the butter-and-cream finish balanced.
