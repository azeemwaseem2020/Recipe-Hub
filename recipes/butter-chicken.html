Butter Chicken Recipe

Butter chicken is at its best when the sauce is silky, gently spiced and deeply savoury rather than simply heavy. This home-style version builds flavour in layers: brown the chicken first, concentrate the tomato base, then finish the curry gently with butter, cream and dried fenugreek.

You do not need restaurant equipment to get a smooth result. The key is to avoid rushing the tomato base and to keep the final simmer gentle after the cream goes in.

20 minPrep
35 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chili powder or paprika
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1/2 tsp dried fenugreek leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander

How to Make Butter Chicken

  1. Marinate: Mix the chicken with yogurt, half the spices and salt. Rest for at least 20 minutes. This gives the chicken time to absorb flavour while you prepare the sauce.
  2. Brown the chicken: Heat a wide pan and add a little oil. Cook the chicken over medium-high heat until the outside develops colour and the pieces are cooked through. Remove them before making the sauce.
  3. Concentrate the tomatoes: In the same pan, cook tomatoes and ginger-garlic paste until soft and reduced. Do not blend while the tomatoes are still watery; concentration gives the finished curry more depth.
  4. Blend the base: Blend the cooked tomato mixture until smooth. If needed, add a small splash of water to help it move through the blender.
  5. Build the curry: Return the sauce to the pan with butter, garam masala and chili powder. Simmer for 8–10 minutes until glossy and slightly thickened.
  6. Finish: Add the chicken and cream. Keep the heat low and simmer for about 5 minutes. Crush dried fenugreek leaves between your palms and stir them in during the final minute.
  7. Serve: Taste for salt, add fresh coriander and take the curry off the heat. Let it sit for a couple of minutes before serving.
RecipeHub Kitchen Tip: If the sauce tastes sharp from the tomatoes, do not immediately add lots of cream. Let it simmer a little longer first; reducing the tomato base usually rounds the flavour more effectively.

Common Mistakes

  • Thin sauce: The tomato base was not reduced enough. Simmer it until it coats the spoon before adding chicken and cream.
  • Curdled cream: Keep the heat low after adding cream and avoid a hard boil.
  • Bland chicken: Season the marinade and sauce separately so the flavour is not concentrated only in the gravy.
  • Burnt spices: Add dry spices with the sauce and butter rather than leaving them alone over high heat.

Easy Variations

  • Use boneless thigh meat for a juicier texture.
  • For a milder family version, replace some chili powder with sweet paprika.
  • For a lighter curry, reduce the cream slightly and loosen the sauce with a little warm water.

Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with butter naan, plain naan, roti or steamed basmati rice. A simple cucumber and onion salad adds freshness and balances the creamy gravy.

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate leftovers in a covered container and use them within a few days. Reheat gently in a pan. If the sauce has thickened in the refrigerator, add a small splash of water or milk and stir over low heat until hot.

Kitchen Tip

Do not boil the curry hard after adding cream. A gentle simmer keeps the sauce smooth and the butter-and-cream finish balanced.
