Chicken Biryani Recipe

Chicken biryani is all about contrast: fluffy, separate rice, tender chicken and a masala that is rich without becoming wet or heavy. This RecipeHub version is designed for a normal home kitchen, with clear timings and practical checkpoints instead of complicated restaurant-style instructions.

The most important detail is rice texture. Stop boiling the rice while the centre still has a little firmness because the final steam will finish the grains. That one step helps prevent the biryani from becoming mushy.

25 minPrep
50 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken, bone-in or boneless
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1–2 tbsp biryani masala, according to taste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander and mint
  • 1 green chili, sliced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

How to Make Chicken Biryani

  1. Prepare the rice: Wash and soak basmati rice for 20 minutes. Drain it before boiling.
  2. Brown the onion: Heat oil in a heavy pot and fry the onion until deep golden. Remove a little fried onion for layering and keep it aside.
  3. Cook the chicken: Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken. Sauté until the chicken changes colour and the raw smell disappears.
  4. Make the masala: Add tomatoes, yogurt, biryani masala, cumin, chili powder, turmeric and salt. Cook until the chicken is tender and the masala is thick. If it looks watery, give it a few extra minutes uncovered.
  5. Parboil the rice: Boil rice in plenty of salted water until it is about 80% cooked. The grain should still have a slight firm centre. Drain straight away.
  6. Layer: Spread the rice over the chicken. Add the reserved fried onion, mint, coriander, green chili and lemon juice. Cover tightly.
  7. Dum cooking: Steam on the lowest heat for about 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and rest for 5 minutes before opening.
  8. Serve: Gently lift the rice from the bottom instead of aggressively stirring. This keeps the long grains intact and distributes the masala naturally.
RecipeHub Kitchen Tip: If you are unsure about the rice, undercook it slightly rather than overcooking it. The trapped steam continues cooking the grains during dum, while overcooked rice cannot be fixed later.

Common Biryani Mistakes

  • Mushy rice: The rice was boiled too long. Stop around 80% cooked and drain immediately.
  • Watery masala: Cook the chicken mixture uncovered until it becomes thick before adding rice.
  • Dry chicken: Avoid cooking the chicken aggressively after it becomes tender. Keep the masala moist enough for the final steam.
  • Weak flavour: Salt the rice water properly and season the chicken masala in stages rather than relying only on the final layer.
  • Broken rice: Do not stir the layered biryani repeatedly. Fluff gently from the edges when serving.

Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with chilled raita, cucumber, lemon wedges and a simple onion salad. Mint chutney also works well if you like extra freshness. For a family meal, pair the biryani with a light salad rather than another heavy curry.

Storage & Reheating

Cool leftovers promptly and keep them covered in the refrigerator. When reheating, sprinkle a small amount of water over the rice, cover it and warm gently until steaming hot. Avoid repeatedly reheating the whole batch; warm only the portion you plan to eat.

Best Result Checklist

Use good basmati rice, salt the boiling water, keep the chicken masala thick, stop the rice at about 80% cooked and give the finished pot a short rest before serving.
