Chicken Biryani Recipe

Chicken biryani is a fragrant rice dish made by layering spiced chicken with basmati rice, herbs and aromatics. This home-style method is designed for a balanced, flavorful pot.

Prep: 25 min · Cook: 50 min · Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp oil and 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Biryani masala, cumin, chili and salt
  • Fresh coriander and mint

How to Make Biryani

  1. Wash and soak the rice for 20 minutes.
  2. Brown the onion in oil. Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken; cook until lightly browned.
  3. Add tomatoes, yogurt and spices. Cover and cook until chicken is tender and the masala thickens.
  4. Boil the rice until about 80% cooked, then drain.
  5. Layer rice over the chicken. Add mint and coriander, cover tightly and steam on low heat for 15 minutes.
  6. Gently fluff the layers and serve hot with raita or salad.
Cooking tip: Do not overcook the rice before layering; slightly firm grains produce better biryani after steaming.
