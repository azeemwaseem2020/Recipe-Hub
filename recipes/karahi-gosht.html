Karahi Gosht Recipe

Karahi gosht is a classic Pakistani meat curry known for a concentrated tomato masala, ginger and green chilies. Use mutton for a traditional result or beef for a heartier version.

Prep:15 min · Cook:40 min · Serves:4

Ingredients

  • 750 g mutton or beef
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1 tsp each cumin and coriander
  • 1 tsp chili powder and salt
  • Fresh ginger and coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil in a heavy karahi and brown the meat.
  2. Add onion and ginger-garlic paste; cook for several minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes, cumin, coriander, chili and salt. Cover and cook until the meat is tender, adding a splash of water when needed.
  4. Cook uncovered until the oil separates and the masala becomes thick.
  5. Garnish with sliced ginger, green chilies and coriander.
Tip: Keep the final masala fairly thick; excessive liquid changes the signature karahi texture.
