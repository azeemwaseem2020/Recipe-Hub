Karahi Gosht Recipe

Karahi gosht gets its character from bhunai—the patient cooking that turns tomato, spice and meat juices into a thick, glossy masala. The finished dish should cling to the meat and carry the aroma of ginger and green chili.

Use mutton for a traditional tender result or beef for a heartier pot. Judge the meat by tenderness rather than the clock alone.

15 minPrep
60 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 750 g mutton or beef
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • Salt
  • Fresh ginger & coriander

How to Make Karahi Gosht

  1. Heat oil in a heavy karahi and brown the meat well.
  2. Add onion and ginger-garlic paste. Cook until fragrant and lightly golden.
  3. Add tomatoes, cumin, coriander, chili and salt. Cover and cook until the meat is tender, adding small amounts of hot water when needed.
  4. Remove the lid and bhuno uncovered until the tomato masala becomes thick and glossy and the oil shows at the edges.
  5. Finish with green chilies, sliced ginger and fresh coriander. Rest briefly before serving.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Add the final green chili and ginger after the bhunai so their fresh aroma stays bright against the deep tomato masala.

Serve

Serve hot with naan, roti or butter naan. Onion rings, lemon and plain yogurt make an excellent fresh side.

Bhunai Tip

The final uncovered cooking removes excess water and concentrates the tomato flavour without needing extra thickener.
