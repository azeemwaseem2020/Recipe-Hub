Chicken Tikka Recipe

Chicken tikka should have a lightly smoky outside, juicy centre and enough spice to make you reach for the lemon. Yogurt tenderises the surface while lemon and ginger-garlic build the base flavour.

For a practical home version, use boneless thigh pieces and cook them quickly over high heat. The little charred spots are not a mistake—they are part of the flavour.

20 minPrep
20 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 700 g boneless chicken
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp chili
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • Onion & lemon

How to Make Chicken Tikka

  1. Cut chicken into even pieces so they cook at the same rate.
  2. Mix yogurt, lemon, ginger-garlic, spices, oil and salt. Coat the chicken thoroughly and marinate for at least one hour.
  3. Heat a grill, oven or heavy pan until very hot. Arrange chicken without crowding it.
  4. Cook, turning occasionally, until the surface has dark little charred spots and the thickest pieces are cooked through.
  5. Rest for a few minutes before serving with lemon and onion.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Reserve a tablespoon of the marinade before adding raw chicken and thin it with yogurt. Use it as a fresh dipping sauce rather than brushing raw marinade back onto cooked chicken.

Serve

Serve with naan, salad, mint chutney or wrap the pieces in paratha with crunchy onion. A squeeze of lemon just before eating makes the spices taste brighter.

Grilling Tip

High heat gives colour before the chicken loses too much moisture. Avoid cooking the pieces slowly in a crowded pan.
