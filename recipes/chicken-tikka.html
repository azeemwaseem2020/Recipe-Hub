Chicken Tikka Recipe

Good chicken tikka has a juicy centre, a lightly smoky edge and enough spice to make fresh lemon taste even brighter. Yogurt helps coat the chicken while lemon, ginger-garlic and warm spices build the flavour.

For a reliable home version, cut the chicken evenly and start with a properly hot cooking surface. High heat gives you colour quickly instead of drying the meat during a long cook.

20 minPrep
20 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 700 g boneless chicken
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Onion, lemon and chutney to serve

How to Make Chicken Tikka

  1. Cut evenly: Trim the chicken and cut it into similar-sized pieces. Even pieces are easier to cook without some drying while others remain undercooked.
  2. Mix marinade: Combine yogurt, lemon juice, ginger-garlic paste, cumin, coriander, garam masala, paprika, chili, oil and salt.
  3. Marinate: Coat every piece and refrigerate for at least one hour. If you have more time, a longer rest gives the surface more flavour.
  4. Heat the cooking surface: Get the grill, oven or heavy pan properly hot before adding chicken. A hot surface helps the marinade colour instead of steaming.
  5. Cook: Arrange pieces with space between them. Turn occasionally and cook until browned with small charred spots and the thickest piece is fully cooked.
  6. Rest: Let the chicken sit for a few minutes so the juices settle before serving.
RecipeHub Kitchen Tip: Reserve a tablespoon of the marinade before adding raw chicken and mix it with a little fresh yogurt for a quick dipping sauce. Never reuse marinade that has touched raw chicken.

Common Mistakes

  • Crowding the pan: Too many pieces release moisture and steam instead of charring.
  • Uneven pieces: Small pieces dry out before large pieces are done.
  • Low heat: A cool cooking surface makes the tikka pale and watery.
  • Overcooking: Once the centre is cooked, remove the pieces rather than waiting for excessive browning.

Easy Variations

  • Use chicken thighs for a naturally juicier result.
  • Add a pinch of smoked paprika for a deeper smoky note when cooking indoors.
  • For a milder family version, reduce the chili and serve extra chutney on the side.

Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with lemon wedges, sliced onion, mint chutney, naan or paratha. The tikka also works well in wraps with crunchy salad and yogurt sauce.

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerate cooked chicken in a covered container and reheat gently in a pan or oven. Avoid repeated reheating because the pieces become dry. A spoonful of yogurt or chutney on the side helps refresh leftovers.

Grilling Tip

High heat gives colour before the chicken loses too much moisture. Leave space around the pieces so the surface can brown.
