Chicken Tikka Recipe

Juicy chicken tikka gets its bold flavor from yogurt, lemon, ginger, garlic and warming spices. It can be grilled, baked or cooked on a hot pan.

Ingredients

  • 750 g boneless chicken
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp paprika or chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Cut chicken into medium pieces and make shallow cuts.
  2. Mix yogurt, lemon juice, ginger-garlic paste, spices, oil and salt.
  3. Coat the chicken and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
  4. Thread onto skewers or arrange on a lined tray.
  5. Cook over high heat, turning as needed, until fully cooked and lightly charred.
  6. Rest briefly and serve with lemon and chutney.

Tips

Do not overcrowd the pan or grill. High heat helps develop the characteristic browned edges while keeping the chicken tender.
