Aloo Gobi Recipe

Aloo gobi is a simple Indian vegetarian dish made with potatoes and cauliflower cooked with turmeric and aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • 500 g cauliflower
  • 400 g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt and coriander

Method

  1. Cut cauliflower and potatoes into even pieces.
  2. Heat oil, add cumin and sauté onion.
  3. Add vegetables and spices and toss well.
  4. Cover and cook on medium-low until tender, stirring occasionally.
  5. Add tomatoes and cook until soft. Finish with garam masala and coriander.

Tip

Do not add too much water; aloo gobi tastes best when the vegetables remain tender rather than watery.
