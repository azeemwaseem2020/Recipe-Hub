Aloo Gobi Recipe

Aloo gobi is at its most delicious when the cauliflower has a little roasted edge and the potatoes stay tender without becoming mushy. A wide pan helps because the vegetables need room to cook rather than steam in their own moisture.

This everyday recipe uses a simple spice base and finishes dry. It works as a quick dinner, lunchbox side or a filling for roti rolls.

15 minPrep
30 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cauliflower
  • 3 potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Ginger & coriander
  • Salt

How to Make Aloo Gobi

  1. Cut potatoes and cauliflower into similar-sized pieces. Keep them dry so they brown better.
  2. Heat oil, add cumin and onion, and cook until lightly golden. Add ginger.
  3. Add potatoes, cauliflower, tomatoes and spices. Toss well and cover.
  4. Cook over medium-low heat until the vegetables are tender, checking occasionally and adding only a small splash of water if necessary.
  5. Remove the lid and cook a few more minutes so the edges become lightly roasted. Finish with garam masala and coriander.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Brown the cauliflower for two minutes on its own before mixing it with the masala. That small extra step gives the finished curry a roasted aroma and keeps the florets more distinct.

Serve

Serve hot with roti, paratha, naan or dal and rice. A little lemon at the table adds freshness.

Texture Tip

Avoid flooding the pan with water. Aloo gobi tastes best when the vegetables cook mostly in their own moisture and finish relatively dry.
