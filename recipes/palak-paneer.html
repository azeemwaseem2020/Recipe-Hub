Palak Paneer Recipe

Palak paneer is at its best when the spinach tastes fresh rather than overcooked and the paneer has a little golden colour on the outside. The secret is speed: blanch the spinach, cool it immediately, and keep the final simmer short.

This version is creamy without being too heavy. A small squeeze of lemon at the end wakes up the spinach and balances the richness of paneer.

15 minPrep
25 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 300 g paneer cubes
  • 500 g fresh spinach
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 green chili
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • Oil & salt
  • Lemon juice

How to Make Palak Paneer

  1. Blanch spinach in boiling water for about 2 minutes. Transfer immediately to cold water, then drain.
  2. Blend spinach with green chili into a smooth puree. Keep it ready beside the stove.
  3. Lightly sear paneer cubes in a little oil until golden. Remove and keep aside.
  4. Cook cumin, onion, ginger-garlic and tomatoes until soft and concentrated. Add turmeric and garam masala.
  5. Add spinach puree and simmer gently for 5–7 minutes. Return paneer and add cream. Finish with lemon and salt.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Sear only half the paneer deeply and leave the rest pale. The curry then has two textures: toasted edges for contrast and soft paneer that absorbs the spinach gravy.

Serve

Serve with naan, roti, jeera rice or plain basmati rice. If making it ahead, keep the curry slightly looser because it thickens as it cools.

Colour Tip

Do not cook spinach puree for a long time. A short final simmer keeps the flavour fresh and the colour more vibrant.
