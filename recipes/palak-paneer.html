Palak Paneer Recipe

Palak paneer combines soft Indian cheese with smooth spinach gravy and aromatic spices. It is a popular vegetarian main course.

Ingredients

  • 500 g spinach
  • 250 g paneer
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 green chili
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp cream
  • 1 tbsp oil and salt

Method

  1. Blanch spinach briefly and cool immediately.
  2. Blend spinach and green chili into a smooth puree.
  3. Sauté cumin, onion and ginger-garlic paste until soft.
  4. Add tomatoes and spices and cook until soft.
  5. Add spinach puree and simmer gently.
  6. Add paneer and cream; cook a few minutes and serve.

Tips

Do not overcook the spinach after blending. Lightly pan-searing paneer before adding it can give extra texture.
