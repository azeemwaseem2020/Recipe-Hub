Nihari Recipe

Nihari is the kind of dish that rewards patience. The meat becomes spoon-tender while the spices settle into a deep, warming gravy. At home, you do not need a huge pot or complicated technique; you need low heat, enough liquid and time.

Start it when the kitchen is quiet and let the gentle simmer do the work. The final flour slurry should thicken the broth lightly, not turn it into a paste.

20 minPrep
3 hrCook
5Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef shank
  • 1 sliced onion
  • 3 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 tbsp nihari masala
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • Hot water as needed
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh ginger & coriander
  • Green chili & lemon

How to Make Nihari

  1. Heat oil in a heavy pot and brown the beef lightly. Add onion and cook until softened.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and nihari masala. Stir for a minute so the spices become fragrant without burning.
  3. Pour in hot water, cover and cook on very low heat until the beef is tender and the broth tastes rich.
  4. Mix flour with cool water until smooth. Add gradually while stirring, then simmer until the gravy lightly coats a spoon.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes and serve with ginger, coriander, green chili and lemon.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Make nihari a day ahead when possible. Chill it overnight, lift off only some of the solidified fat, then reheat slowly. The flavour becomes rounder while the broth remains rich.

Serving

Serve piping hot with naan, kulcha or fresh roti. Lemon and ginger are not just decoration: their freshness cuts through the richness and makes each spoonful feel balanced.

Kitchen Tip

Keep the simmer gentle. A hard boil can make the meat fibres tighten and the gravy cloudy.
