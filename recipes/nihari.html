Pakistani Nihari Recipe

Nihari is a rich Pakistani slow-cooked stew traditionally prepared with beef or other meat and a fragrant spice blend. Slow cooking gives the meat a tender texture and the gravy its deep flavor.

Prep:20 min · Cook:160 min · Serves:5

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef shank or stewing beef
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 tbsp nihari masala
  • 2 tbsp flour mixed with water
  • Salt
  • Fresh ginger, coriander and lemon

Method

  1. Heat oil and brown the meat with onion.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and nihari masala; stir for a minute.
  3. Add enough hot water to cover the meat. Cover and simmer gently until very tender.
  4. Stir flour into cold water until smooth, then add gradually while stirring.
  5. Simmer another 15–20 minutes until glossy and thick.
  6. Serve with naan and sliced ginger, coriander and lemon.
Tip: Low, steady heat is the key to tender meat and a well-developed nihari gravy.
