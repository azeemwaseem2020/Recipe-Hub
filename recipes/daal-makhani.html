Daal Makhani Recipe

Daal makhani is not meant to be rushed. Black lentils and kidney beans become creamy when cooked gently, while the final butter-and-cream finish should round the flavour rather than cover it.

For a practical home version, focus on fully tender lentils, a concentrated tomato base and a slow final simmer. The result is rich, silky and deeply comforting.

15 minPrep
90 minCook
5Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole black urad dal
  • 1/4 cup kidney beans
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • Dried fenugreek
  • Salt

How to Make Daal Makhani

  1. Soak the lentils and kidney beans, then cook until completely tender.
  2. Cook cumin, onion and ginger-garlic until fragrant. Add tomato and spices and cook until thick.
  3. Stir in the cooked lentils with a little cooking liquid. Simmer gently and stir occasionally.
  4. Mash a portion of the lentils against the side of the pot to create natural creaminess.
  5. Add butter, cream and crushed dried fenugreek. Simmer gently for 5–10 minutes and adjust salt.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Make it slightly thicker than needed, then loosen individual servings with a spoonful of hot cooking water. The flavour stays concentrated while the texture remains silky.

Serve

Serve with naan, roti or basmati rice. A little coriander and a small knob of butter on top are enough for a beautiful finish.

Make-Ahead Tip

Daal makhani often tastes better after resting. Reheat gently and add hot water only if the gravy has tightened.
