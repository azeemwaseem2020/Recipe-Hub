Daal Makhani Recipe

Daal makhani is a rich Indian lentil dish made with whole black urad dal, kidney beans, tomato, butter and cream. Slow cooking gives it a deep, silky texture.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole black urad dal
  • 1/4 cup kidney beans
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Soak dal and kidney beans overnight.
  2. Boil until very tender.
  3. Cook onion, ginger-garlic paste and tomato with spices.
  4. Add lentils and simmer gently until creamy.
  5. Stir in butter and cream and cook a few minutes.

Tips

Slow simmering is the key to a creamy texture. Add a little hot water if the dal becomes too thick while cooking.
