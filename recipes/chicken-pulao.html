Chicken Pulao Recipe

Unlike biryani, pulao gets much of its character from the broth itself. The rice cooks directly in a seasoned chicken stock, so every grain absorbs the aroma of cinnamon, cardamom, cumin and onion.

The practical rule is simple: taste the broth before adding the rice. It should be slightly more seasoned than you want the finished rice to taste because the grains will absorb that seasoning.

15 minPrep
40 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Cinnamon & cardamom
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 4 cups stock or water
  • 2 green chilies
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt & coriander

How to Make Chicken Pulao

  1. Rinse rice until the water is mostly clear and soak for 15–20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil and brown onion with cumin, cinnamon, cardamom and bay leaf. Add ginger-garlic and chicken.
  3. Pour in stock or water and simmer until chicken is tender. Taste and adjust salt.
  4. Add drained rice and green chilies. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat when most liquid is absorbed.
  5. Cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat for 10 minutes. Rest 5 minutes before fluffing.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Save half a cup of the chicken broth before adding rice. If the rice looks dry near the end, a few tablespoons of hot broth restore moisture without making the grains mushy.

Serve

Serve with raita, salad, achar or a simple mint chutney. It is especially good as a family lunch because the whole meal comes together in one pot.

Rice Tip

Measure the liquid carefully and let the pulao rest before opening the lid. Resting finishes the steam cooking and helps the grains separate.
