Chicken Pulao Recipe

Chicken pulao is a fragrant Pakistani rice dish made with basmati rice, tender chicken and whole spices. It is simpler and lighter than many layered rice dishes.

Ingredients

  • 750 g chicken
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste
  • 5 cups water
  • Fresh coriander

Method

  1. Wash rice and soak for 20 minutes.
  2. Fry onion in oil until golden.
  3. Add whole spices, ginger-garlic paste and chicken; cook briefly.
  4. Add water and salt and simmer until chicken is nearly tender.
  5. Add drained rice and cook until most water is absorbed.
  6. Cover and steam on low heat until the rice is fluffy and fully cooked.

Tips

Use good-quality aged basmati rice and measure the cooking liquid carefully. Let the pulao rest covered for 10 minutes before fluffing.
