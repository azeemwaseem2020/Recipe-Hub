Crispy Samosa Recipe

These classic South Asian samosas have a crisp pastry shell and a warmly spiced potato and pea filling. They are ideal for tea time, Ramadan gatherings and family snacks.

Prep:25 min · Cook:25 min · Makes:12

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • Salt and oil for frying

Method

  1. Mix flour, salt and a little oil. Add water gradually and make a firm dough. Rest 20 minutes.
  2. Mix mashed potatoes, peas, cumin, coriander, chili and salt.
  3. Divide dough, roll thin, cut and fold each piece into a cone.
  4. Fill the cone, seal the edge with water and repeat.
  5. Fry on medium-low heat until crisp and golden. Drain well.
Tip: Starting samosas in moderately warm oil helps the pastry cook through and develop a crisp shell.
