Crispy Samosa Recipe

Golden, flaky samosas filled with spiced potato and peas. This home-friendly method focuses on a firm dough, balanced filling and the slow frying technique that keeps the shell crisp.

Prep 25 minCook 25 minMakes 12

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 3 boiled potatoes
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp crushed coriander
  • 1/2 tsp fennel
  • 1 green chili
  • Salt
  • Oil for frying

Why this samosa works

A homemade samosa is worth the shaping time because the pastry can be thin and crisp while the filling stays generous. The key is a firm dough. Rub oil into the flour before adding water; the mixture should feel slightly crumbly, not soft like bread dough. That little detail helps create the classic short, blistered shell. Keep the potato filling slightly chunky so every bite has texture. Toasting cumin, coriander and fennel for a few seconds before mixing them in gives the filling a warmer aroma.

When folding, do not overfill the cone. A smaller amount seals more neatly and prevents leaks during frying. Start in moderately hot oil and fry slowly. Very hot oil can brown the outside before the pastry cooks through. Once the shell is firm, raise the heat slightly for the final golden colour. Serve with mint chutney and tamarind sauce.

Unique RecipeHub idea: Add a pinch of crushed fennel and a few drops of lemon to the potato filling for a fresh, fragrant finish.

Step-by-step method

  1. Mix flour, salt and 2 tablespoons oil. Add water gradually and make a firm dough. Cover and rest 20 minutes.
  2. Mash potatoes roughly. Mix with peas, cumin, coriander, fennel, chili and salt.
  3. Divide dough, roll each portion, cut into a strip and form a cone. Fill lightly and seal the edge.
  4. Heat oil over medium-low heat. Fry in small batches until crisp and golden, turning gently.
  5. Drain on a rack or paper and serve hot with chutney.
Practical tip: If the samosas colour too quickly, lower the heat. A crisp shell should develop gradually.

Serving & storage

Serve with mint chutney, tamarind chutney, chai or yogurt dip. Shaped uncooked samosas can be frozen in a single layer and cooked from chilled.
