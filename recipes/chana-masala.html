Chana Masala Recipe

Chana masala is a comforting Indian chickpea curry with a tangy tomato base and warm spices. It works well with rice, roti or naan.

Prep:10 min · Cook:30 min · Serves:4

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked chickpeas
  • 2 tomatoes and 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp each cumin and coriander
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp chili powder
  • Salt, coriander and lemon

Method

  1. Cook onion until golden, then add ginger-garlic paste.
  2. Add tomatoes and spices. Cook until tomatoes soften and the masala becomes thick.
  3. Add chickpeas and enough water for a light gravy. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Mash a few chickpeas to naturally thicken the curry.
  5. Finish with coriander and lemon juice.
Tip: A short simmer after adding chickpeas lets the spices penetrate without turning the beans mushy.
