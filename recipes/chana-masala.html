Chana Masala Recipe

Chana masala is a pantry-friendly curry that becomes memorable when the tomato masala is cooked properly. The onion should soften and caramelise, the tomatoes should lose their raw edge, and the chickpeas should simmer long enough to absorb the spices.

This is an everyday recipe with a small restaurant-style touch: mash a few chickpeas directly into the gravy. It makes the curry naturally thick and helps the masala cling to every bite.

15 minPrep
30 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked chickpeas
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Amchur or lemon
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt & coriander

How to Make Chana Masala

  1. Heat oil and cumin. Add onion and cook until golden and soft.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste, then tomatoes and spices. Cook patiently until the tomato mixture becomes thick and the raw smell disappears.
  3. Add chickpeas and enough water for a thick curry. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  4. Mash a few chickpeas with the back of a spoon. Stir them through the gravy.
  5. Add garam masala and amchur or lemon. Taste for salt and finish with coriander.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Toast the cumin for 20 seconds before adding onion, then reserve a pinch of fresh coriander and lemon for the very last minute. That contrast gives a simple chickpea curry a brighter, street-food feel.

Serve

Serve with roti, naan, paratha or basmati rice. For a weekend brunch, pair it with sliced onion, yogurt and a little green chutney.

Texture Tip

The gravy should cling to the chickpeas. If it looks watery, simmer uncovered for a few extra minutes before finishing.
