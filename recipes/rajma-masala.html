Rajma Masala Recipe

Rajma masala is one of those dishes that feels better the longer it sits on the table. Tender kidney beans soak up a tomato-and-onion gravy, while cumin and garam masala give it the warm aroma people expect from a proper rajma chawal dinner.

The most useful home-cooking trick is to make the masala thick before adding the beans. That way the beans carry flavour instead of simply floating in tomato water.

15 minPrep
35 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked kidney beans
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 2 tomatoes or puree
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • Coriander & lemon

How to Make Rajma Masala

  1. Heat oil and cumin. Add onion and cook until golden at the edges.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste, then tomato and the powdered spices. Cook until the mixture is thick and the oil starts to show around the sides.
  3. Add cooked kidney beans and enough water to make a spoon-coating curry. Simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  4. Mash a few beans against the side of the pot. This naturally thickens the gravy without extra flour.
  5. Finish with garam masala, coriander and a squeeze of lemon. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Keep one small cup of whole rajma aside, mash the rest lightly and return the whole beans at the end. You get a creamy gravy with distinct, satisfying beans in every serving.

Serve

Serve with hot basmati rice, especially simple jeera rice, plus sliced onion and yogurt. Rajma also reheats beautifully, so it is a smart make-ahead lunch.

Kitchen Tip

If using dried kidney beans, soak and cook them until completely tender before making the curry. Properly soft beans make the final texture much better.
