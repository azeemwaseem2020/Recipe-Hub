Rajma Masala Recipe

Rajma masala is a hearty Indian kidney bean curry with a rich tomato and spice base. Serve it with rice for a satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked kidney beans
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt and fresh coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil and temper cumin.
  2. Add onion and cook until lightly golden. Add ginger-garlic paste.
  3. Add tomato puree and spices; cook until the oil separates.
  4. Add kidney beans and a little water. Simmer gently.
  5. Mash a few beans to thicken the gravy and finish with garam masala.

Serving

Serve hot with steamed basmati rice, jeera rice or roti.
