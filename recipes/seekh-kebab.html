Seekh Kebab Recipe

Good seekh kebab is juicy in the middle, browned at the edges and fragrant with cumin, coriander and green chili. The biggest home-kitchen challenge is usually moisture: too much onion or wet herbs make the mixture slide off the skewer.

This method keeps the mixture firm enough to shape while protecting the tender texture. Chill it before cooking and use high heat for quick browning.

20 minPrep
15 minCook
8Kebabs

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef or lamb
  • 1 small finely chopped onion
  • 2 green chilies
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Lemon, salt & oil

How to Make Seekh Kebab

  1. Wrap chopped onion in a clean cloth or paper towel and squeeze out as much moisture as possible.
  2. Mix mince with onion, chilies, coriander, ginger-garlic, spices, lemon and salt. Mix until the texture becomes slightly sticky.
  3. Cover and chill for 30 minutes. This makes shaping much easier.
  4. Press portions firmly around skewers. If using a pan, shape into long logs with wet hands.
  5. Cook over high heat, turning carefully, until browned and cooked through. Brush lightly with oil if needed.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Add a tiny pinch of dried fenugreek leaves to the mince. It gives a gentle smoky, restaurant-style aroma without overpowering the kebab.

Serve

Serve immediately with naan, mint chutney, sliced onion and lemon. You can also tuck the kebab into a warm paratha with yogurt and salad for a quick homemade roll.

Juiciness Tip

Do not repeatedly press the kebabs while they cook. Let the surface brown and turn them only when they release naturally from the pan or grill.
