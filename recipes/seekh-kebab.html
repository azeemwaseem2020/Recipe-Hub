Seekh Kebab Recipe

Pakistani seekh kebabs are seasoned minced meat shaped around skewers and grilled until browned, juicy and aromatic.

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef or lamb
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green chilies
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and oil

Method

  1. Mix minced meat with onion, chilies, coriander, ginger-garlic paste and spices.
  2. Chill the mixture for 20 minutes.
  3. Shape around skewers into long kebabs.
  4. Brush with oil and grill, turning regularly.
  5. Cook until browned and fully cooked. Serve with lemon and chutney.

Tips

Chill the mixture before shaping and keep the heat high enough to brown the outside without drying the kebab.
