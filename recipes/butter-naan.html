Butter Naan Recipe

Soft, lightly chewy naan made at home and finished with butter. This Pakistani and Indian bread pairs well with curries and grilled dishes.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup plain yogurt
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Butter for brushing

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and salt.
  2. Add yogurt, warm water and oil and make a soft dough.
  3. Knead until smooth. Cover and rest for 20 minutes.
  4. Divide and roll into naan shapes.
  5. Cook on a very hot skillet until puffed and browned, turning once.
  6. Brush generously with butter and serve hot.

Tips

Keep the skillet very hot, avoid making the naan too thick, and brush with butter immediately after cooking.
