Butter Naan Recipe

Soft naan at home depends more on dough feel and pan heat than on complicated ingredients. The dough should be soft and slightly tacky, then rested long enough to relax. A properly heated tawa creates the quick bubbles and toasted spots that make naan feel special.

Brush the bread with butter while it is still hot so the surface stays soft and fragrant.

60 minRest & prep
15 minCook
6Naan

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Butter
  • Coriander or nigella seeds

How to Make Butter Naan

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add yogurt, oil and warm water gradually to make a soft dough.
  2. Knead briefly until smooth, cover and rest for about an hour.
  3. Divide into six balls. Roll each into an oval, keeping the centre slightly thicker than the edges.
  4. Heat a tawa until very hot. Lightly dampen one side of the naan, place that side down and cook until bubbles appear and the base is spotted.
  5. Flip and finish cooking. Remove immediately and brush generously with butter.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Mix a little finely chopped coriander into softened butter and brush it over the hot naan. The herb releases its aroma instantly and gives a fresh finish without changing the dough.

Serve

Serve straight from the tawa with butter chicken, karahi gosht, nihari or a simple lentil curry. Keep cooked naan wrapped in a clean towel so it stays soft.

Heat Tip

If the tawa is only warm, the naan dries before bubbles develop. Preheat properly and cook each naan quickly.
