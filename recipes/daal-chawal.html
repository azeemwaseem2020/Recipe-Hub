Daal Chawal Recipe

Daal chawal is simple food, but the right tadka can make the whole kitchen smell like home. The lentils should be soft and spoonable, the rice fluffy, and the garlic-cumin tempering fragrant without tasting burnt.

This is an easy weekday meal that works best when the daal and rice are cooked separately. That way you can control the texture of each part and serve them together at the table.

10 minPrep
35 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup lentils
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • Chili flakes and fresh coriander
  • Salt to taste

How to Make Daal Chawal

  1. Cook the daal: Rinse the lentils and cook with turmeric, salt and enough water until completely tender. Add a little hot water later if you prefer a lighter consistency.
  2. Cook the rice: Rinse basmati rice until the water is less cloudy. Cook separately in salted water until the grains are tender and fluffy. Drain if needed and let them steam for a few minutes.
  3. Prepare the tadka: Heat ghee or oil in a small pan. Add cumin, sliced garlic and chili flakes. Cook just until the garlic turns golden and aromatic.
  4. Finish the daal: Pour the hot tadka into the cooked lentils carefully. Stir and simmer for about 2 minutes so the flavours combine.
  5. Balance the texture: Taste for salt and adjust the daal with hot water if it is too thick. Keep the rice separate so each grain stays fluffy.
  6. Serve: Spoon the daal beside or over the rice and finish with coriander and fresh lemon.
RecipeHub Kitchen Tip: Keep one spoonful of the fresh tadka aside and drizzle it over each plate at the table. The aroma stays much stronger than when all of it is cooked into the pot.

Common Mistakes

  • Mushy rice: Do not overcook basmati. Let it steam after draining instead of stirring repeatedly.
  • Watery daal: Simmer uncovered for a few extra minutes to concentrate it.
  • Burnt tadka: Garlic should be golden, not dark brown. Remove the pan from heat if it colours too quickly.
  • Flat flavour: Taste the daal before and after the tadka so salt can be balanced at the end.

Serving Suggestions

Serve with achar, sliced onion, cucumber, yogurt and lemon. A little green chili and coriander chutney is also excellent with the soft lentils and rice.

Storage & Reheating

Store daal and rice separately in covered containers in the refrigerator. Reheat the daal with a splash of water and warm the rice gently so it does not dry out. Add fresh lemon or a small tadka when serving leftovers.

Home Tip

Cook the rice separately from the daal. It gives you much better control over fluffy rice and creamy lentils.
