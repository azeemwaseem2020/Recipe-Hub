Daal Chawal Recipe

Daal chawal is simple food, but a good tadka can make the whole kitchen smell like home. The lentils should be soft and spoonable, the rice fluffy, and the final garlic-cumin tempering hot enough to bloom the spices without burning them.

This recipe is practical for a weekday meal and easy to adjust. Add more water for a lighter daal or simmer uncovered for a thicker texture.

10 minPrep
35 minCook
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup lentils
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • Chili flakes & coriander
  • Salt

How to Make Daal Chawal

  1. Rinse lentils and cook with turmeric, salt and enough water until completely tender.
  2. Rinse basmati rice and cook separately with salted water until the grains are fluffy. Drain if needed.
  3. For the tadka, heat ghee or oil. Add cumin, sliced garlic and chili flakes. Cook until the garlic is golden, not dark.
  4. Stir the tadka into the hot daal. Simmer for 2 minutes and adjust the consistency with hot water.
  5. Serve over rice with coriander and a squeeze of lemon.
RecipeHub Unique Idea: Keep one spoonful of the fresh tadka aside and drizzle it over each plate at the table. The aroma stays much stronger than when all the tadka is cooked into the pot.

Serve

Serve with achar, sliced onion, cucumber, yogurt and lemon. It is also excellent with a simple green chili and coriander chutney.

Home Tip

Taste the daal before the tadka goes in. Salt and acidity are easier to balance while the lentils are still plain.
