Daal Chawal Recipe

Daal chawal is one of Pakistan's most comforting everyday meals: simple lentils with aromatic tempering served over fluffy rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yellow lentils
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • Salt and fresh coriander

Method

  1. Cook washed lentils with turmeric, salt and water until soft.
  2. Prepare basmati rice separately until fluffy.
  3. Heat oil and fry onion, garlic, cumin and chili until fragrant.
  4. Pour the hot tempering over the dal.
  5. Serve dal with rice and fresh coriander.

Serving idea

Serve with achar, salad, lemon wedges or plain yogurt for a classic Pakistani meal.
